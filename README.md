## 🧭 About me

I am a **Mechanical Engineer**, **Electrical Engineer**, and **Clinical Engineering Specialist**, as well as a **co-owner of Biogêneses Technology**.

My work connects the physical and digital sides of engineering: sensors, electronic circuits, embedded control, communication networks, backend services, and user interfaces. I develop solutions for **temperature-sensitive storage, healthcare equipment monitoring, data acquisition, and automation**.

My multidisciplinary background helps me look beyond a single circuit or application. I consider the equipment, its operating environment, the people using it, and what happens when a sensor fails, connectivity drops, or the power supply changes.

**My focus: practical engineering that connects hardware, firmware, and software into a complete working system.**

## 🧠 Core engineering competencies

### Embedded firmware & control

**STM32, ESP32, ESP32-S3, and AVR/ATmega** development with C and C++. My work includes state machines, non-blocking timing, sensor acquisition and filtering, peripheral integration, persistent configuration, display interfaces, alarm management, and equipment control sequences.

I pay particular attention to **controlled startup, watchdog supervision, communication timeouts, operating-state transitions, and behavior during power or connectivity interruptions**.

### Electronics, instrumentation & product integration

Schematic development and PCB layout with **KiCad**, component selection, analog measurement, sensor interfaces, voltage regulation, battery monitoring, and protection of external interfaces.

I combine electronic design with an understanding of **mechanical integration, refrigeration, thermal behavior, and equipment maintenance**, keeping assembly, diagnostics, and serviceability in view.

### Connected systems & software

Integration from the device to the application: **serial communication, industrial interfaces, Ethernet, Wi-Fi, APIs, databases, dashboards, and automated notifications**.

My work also includes **Linux/VPS deployment, service integration, and n8n workflows** for reporting, support, and operational automation.

### Clinical & electrical engineering

Technical work involving **healthcare equipment, fault diagnosis, maintenance, electrical measurements, power-quality analysis, thermographic inspection, and engineering reports**.

This field experience informs my development work: useful systems must be understandable, diagnosable, and maintainable—not just functional on the bench.

## 📐 How I approach a project

```text
Understand the equipment and its operating constraints
                        ↓
Design the electronics and system architecture
                        ↓
Develop firmware and validate control behavior
                        ↓
Integrate communication, data, and user interfaces
                        ↓
Test in the field, document, and refine
```

**The questions I keep asking:** What happens when something fails? How will the technician diagnose it? Can the operator understand it? Can it be maintained and improved?
